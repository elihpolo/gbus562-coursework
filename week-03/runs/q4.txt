ANSWER
No — a member service representative cannot approve this refund alone.

The outgoing international wire transfer fee is $45 (consumer-fee-schedule.html, "Wire transfers"). A representative may only approve refunds of "a single fee of $30 or less without anyone else's approval" (fee-refund-guide.html, "What a member service representative can approve"). Since $45 exceeds that threshold, it falls under the rule that "a refund needs the branch manager's approval when the fee is more than $30" (fee-refund-guide.html, "Refunds that need a branch manager").

So this refund requires branch manager approval — unless other conditions apply that would escalate it further:
- If the member has already had a refund in the last 90 days, or is requesting several fees refunded at once, branch manager approval is still the trigger named in that section (fee-refund-guide.html, "Refunds that need a branch manager").
- If this refund would bring the member's total refunds in the last 90 days above $150, or if the account is a business account, approval from the Member Services Director is required instead (fee-refund-guide.html, "Refunds that need the Member Services Director").

The documents do not state which of these additional conditions apply in your case, so you should check the member's refund history and account type before determining whether branch manager or Director approval is needed. Whatever is approved must also be recorded in the account notes with the fee, amount, reason, and approver's name (fee-refund-guide.html, "Recording a refund").
