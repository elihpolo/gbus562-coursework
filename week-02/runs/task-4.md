### System

### You
Wildframe Coffee Roasters is planning a four-week pop-up stand this spring. We can only choose one location, and our total rent budget is $5,000 for all four weeks.

I want you to compare these three locations using the data below:

Riverside Farmers Market: 9,000 visitors per week; 2% of visitors buy; average sale $14 (mostly whole-bean bags); rent $900 per week.

Union Station concourse: 30,000 visitors per week; 0.5% of visitors buy; average sale $6 (mostly single cups); rent $2,000 per week.

Campus library atrium: 6,000 visitors per week; 3% of visitors buy; average sale $5 (single cups); rent $600 per week.

Work through the calculations step by step. For each location, calculate expected weekly customers, weekly revenue, total four-week rent, and estimated four-week revenue minus rent.

Then compare the locations, explain which ones meet our $5,000 rent budget, and recommend the best option based on the numbers.

End your response with one final line that starts with Recommendation: followed by only the location name.

# Wildframe Pop-Up Location Analysis

Let's run the numbers on all three spots before we commit a month of our life (and our rent budget) to one location.

## Riverside Farmers Market

- **Weekly customers:** 9,000 × 2% = **180 customers**
- **Weekly revenue:** 180 × $14 = **$2,520**
- **4-week rent:** $900 × 4 = **$3,600**
- **4-week revenue:** $2,520 × 4 = **$10,080**
- **Revenue minus rent:** $10,080 − $3,600 = **$6,480**

## Union Station Concourse

- **Weekly customers:** 30,000 × 0.5% = **150 customers**
- **Weekly revenue:** 150 × $6 = **$900**
- **4-week rent:** $2,000 × 4 = **$8,000**
- **4-week revenue:** $900 × 4 = **$3,600**
- **Revenue minus rent:** $3,600 − $8,000 = **−$4,400**

## Campus Library Atrium

- **Weekly customers:** 6,000 × 3% = **180 customers**
- **Weekly revenue:** 180 × $5 = **$900**
- **4-week rent:** $600 × 4 = **$2,400**
- **4-week revenue:** $900 × 4 = **$3,600**
- **Revenue minus rent:** $3,600 − $2,400 = **$1,200**

## Budget Check ($5,000 total)

| Location | 4-Week Rent | Fits Budget? |
|---|---|---|
| Riverside Farmers Market | $3,600 | ✅ Yes |
| Union Station Concourse | $8,000 | ❌ No — blows budget by $3,000 |
| Campus Library Atrium | $2,400 | ✅ Yes |

Union Station is out of the running immediately — it's over budget *and* it loses money. High foot traffic means nothing if almost nobody stops for coffee.

## The Real Comparison: Riverside vs. Campus

Both fit comfortably under $5,000, so it comes down to pure performance:

- **Riverside** nets **$6,480** — more than 5x Campus Library's profit
- **Campus Library** nets **$1,200** — fine, but modest

Riverside wins because the whole-bean bags (at $14 a pop) do more heavy lifting than single cups, even with a similar conversion-adjusted customer count to Campus. More revenue per transaction beats more foot traffic every time here.

## Bottom Line

Riverside Farmers Market gives us the best return, stays well under budget (leaving $1,400 of our $5,000 unspent, if we want to put it toward signage, samples, or that fancy new grinder), and plays to our strength: selling bags, not just cups.

**Recommendation: Riverside Farmers Market**
