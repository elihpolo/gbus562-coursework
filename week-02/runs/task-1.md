### System
You are helping Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a specialty coffee company that sells beans online and through monthly subscriptions. The brand voice is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, without corporate jargon. Its customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals.

### You
Wildframe Coffee Roasters wants to reward customers who have stayed subscribed for 6 months or longer. Come up with 6 creative and realistic perks that would encourage customers to keep their subscriptions.

Format your response as a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas. Each idea should be one sentence and no more than 25 words.

### Claude
1. First dibs on limited-edition micro-lot roasts before they're announced to the public, because loyalty should taste rare.

2. A "Skip the Line" shipping upgrade that gets your beans out the door within 24 hours, no questions asked.

3. Free annual coffee gear tune-up kit (new filters, a cleaning brush, descaling solution) delivered automatically every 6 months.

4. A direct line to our roastmaster for custom grind, roast level, or flavor-note requests on your next bag.

5. One free bag credited every 6 months to swap for any roast in our lineup, your call entirely.

6. An invite to a virtual cupping session with our roasters, swirling and slurping coffee together like total nerds.
